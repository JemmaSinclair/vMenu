Where Your Data Goes & Categories

Saved items with existing custom categories keep their category names. Uncategorized items are automatically filed under an "Imported from old vMenu" category so they are easy to find and organize.

Name Collision Safety

If a saved vehicle, character, ped, or loadout has the exact same name as an existing save in vMenu.Enhanced, the incoming item will be appended with (Legacy) (e.g. Adder (Legacy)) so no existing data is lost.

    Differences & Format Adjustments

    • DLC Clothing: Multiplayer clothing drawables are matched by numerical ID. Due to GTA V game build updates, some cloth indices may shift. Re-saving your character once in-game locks them in.
    • Normal Ped Multiplayer Saves: Multiplayer peds previously saved under "Saved Peds" rather than "Custom Characters" lack head blend data and will appear with default faces.
    • Custom Weapon Attachments: Weapon components not installed on this server cannot be loaded and will be omitted.
    • Obsolete Settings: Retired legacy settings with no equivalent in vMenu.Enhanced are omitted.
